About GAMMA

GAMMA was founded at Brown in 2012. We teach, compete, and win in striking and grappling.

Our Disciplines

Striking

Master the art of striking with our comprehensive training in boxing, kickboxing, and Muay Thai techniques.

Submission Grappling

Learn the fundamentals of grappling, wrestling, and submission techniques without the traditional gi.
